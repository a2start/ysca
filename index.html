RESIDENTIAL FACILITY

Train Here. Stay Focused. Develop Faster.

Our academy hostel provides a safe, comfortable and disciplined living environment for outstation players who want to train full-time with Young Star Cricket Academy without the stress and time loss of daily commuting. Located near the training grounds in Greater Noida, the hostel allows players to fully immerse themselves in structured cricket development.

  • Twin- and triple-sharing accommodation
  • Three meals plus an evening snack
  • Resident warden, security and CCTV coverage
  • Training, recovery, study and rest in a balanced routine

Sports-Specific Conditioning
FITNESS & CONDITIONING

Cricket Fitness & Conditioning

Build the athlete behind the cricketer. Modern competitive cricket demands players who are physically exceptional — fast bowlers needing explosive power, batsmen needing rotational core torque, and fielders needing agility and throwing speed.

  • Strength training for powerful bat-speed & bowling pace
  • Speed, ladder drills & quick deceleration mechanics
  • Cricket-specific stamina to endure long tournament matches
  • Shoulder, hip & spine mobility to reduce injury risk

Foundation, Junior and Senior pathways

Foundation introduces cricket to ages 6–10. Junior Development builds technique and match awareness for ages 11–14. Senior Performance is the 15–19 pathway for more focused cricket discussion.
