# YSCA SEO 30/60/90-day roadmap

## Days 0–30: establish trust and measurement

- Completed in source: safe form prefills, no-false-success storage handling, basic rate limiting and removal of high-visibility unverified copy claims. The remaining account-level and fact-verification actions below are still required.
- Approve one fact sheet for NAP, programme details, facilities, coaches, trial policy, fees and service area.
- Bind and test `CONTACTS_KV` on the Pages preview; establish an owner notification and retention process before advertising the form as live.
- Verify the intended `.com` domain in Google Search Console, submit the canonical sitemap and check coverage/canonical reports after deployment.
- Confirm or create the real Google Business Profile; use the approved NAP, primary category, training-ground location, hours, official website and current photos. Do not create duplicate listings or reviews.
- Replace generic schema `sameAs` links only with verified official profiles.

## Days 31–60: publish confirmed visitor-helpful information

- Use Search Console query/page data and Keyword Planner ideas to update `seo-research/keyword-research.csv`; retain source, date, location setting and reported ranges.
- With owner approval, revise programme, facility, hostel and coach copy to answer actual parent/player questions: eligibility, session format, equipment, fees/timings, trial process and contact route.
- Add page-specific Open Graph/Twitter metadata and factual structured data where it reflects visible content.
- Publish only genuinely useful, academy-authored updates such as a verified admissions guide, a first-session checklist or a local travel/parking note.

## Days 61–90: improve local authority and iterate

- Review Search Console impressions, clicks, queries, pages and indexed canonical URLs; prioritise pages with relevant impressions but weak clicks or mismatched intent.
- Ask real families for honest Google reviews through an appropriate, non-incentivised process. Never write, buy or gate reviews.
- Maintain current photos and accurate business details on the website and Google Business Profile.
- Seek relevant, earned local mentions from schools, cricket organisations, tournaments or community partners only where a real relationship/activity exists.
- Re-run staging performance, accessibility and conversion QA after material releases; document changes and compare enquiry quality rather than promising rankings.

## Success measures

Track Search Console clicks/impressions/CTR, indexed canonical URLs, Google Business Profile calls/directions/website actions, form success/error rate, call/WhatsApp enquiries and qualified admissions conversations. Do not treat rankings alone as a success metric, and do not promise a first-page position or a fixed timeline.
