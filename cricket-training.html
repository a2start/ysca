Cricket Training & Coaching Grounds

Comprehensive cricket coaching on BCCI-standard turf wickets, full-size match grounds, and all-weather practice tracks supervised by certified coaching mentors in Greater Noida.

Train With Precision. Build Champion Mindset.

At Young Star Cricket Academy (YSCA), cricket training is treated as a science as much as an art. Our training ecosystem combines rigorous technical biomechanics, situational match play simulations, and athletic discipline to groom players capable of competing at district, state, and national age-group tournaments.

Our expansive cricket grounds feature multiple natural turf pitches prepared with professional clay soil, floodlit open-wicket practice zones, and cutting-edge bowling machine tracks. Trainees benefit from structured technical batting drills, pace and spin bowling masterclasses, and high-intensity fielding circuits.

Whether starting out in grassroots cricket or preparing for competitive Ranji and state trials, our NIS & BCCI certified coaching panel provides structured, measurable, and personalized development pathways.

Pillars of Our Cricket Training Architecture

Batting Technical Mastery

Grip alignment, head stillness, balance, front and back-foot strokeplay, shot selection under pressure, and power-hitting technique.

Pace & Seam Craft

Run-up consistency, gather and leap alignment, upright wrist seam presentation, reverse swing, and bouncer/yorker accuracy.

Spin Bowling Artistry

Finger and wrist spin revolutions, flight and drift control, subtle variations (arm ball, googly, carrom ball), and bowling to strategic fields.

Dynamic Athletic Fielding

Direct-hit target drills, slip and close-in catching reflexes, boundary diving, relay throwing, and fast ground pick-and-release mechanics.

Biomechanical Video Analysis

High-speed slow-motion cameras tracking batting elbow angles, bowling release height, and front-foot landing to rectify technical flaws.

Match Temperament & Tactics

Weekly inter-academy red-ball and white-ball matches, chase pressure scenarios, field captaincy drills, and psychological resilience coaching.

Weekly Training Structure

A balanced blend of technical instruction, net volume, match scenarios, and recovery.

Monday Batting Biomechanics & Top-Hand Drills + Pace Bowling Alignment Nets
Tuesday Spin Bowling Flight & Drift + Range Hitting & Bowling Machine Drills
Wednesday High-Intensity Fielding Obstacle Circuits, Direct Hits & Slip Catching
Thursday Open Wicket Match Simulations & Situational Target Chasing (20/40 Overs)
Friday Technical Video Review Analysis + Individual 1-on-1 Corrective Net Batches
Saturday Full-Dress Inter-Academy League Practice Matches & Umpired Scenarios
Sunday Active Recovery, Mobility Screening & Optional Masterclass Throwdowns
