RESIDENTIAL FACILITY

Academy Hostel — Greater Noida

A safe, disciplined and focused residential facility for outstation cricket players committed to full-time training at Young Star Cricket Academy.

Train Here. Stay Focused. Develop Faster.

Our academy hostel provides a safe, comfortable and disciplined living environment for outstation players who want to train full-time with Young Star Cricket Academy without the stress and time loss of daily commuting. Located near the training grounds in Greater Noida, the hostel allows players to fully immerse themselves in structured cricket development.

Residential players benefit from a structured daily routine that balances cricket training, fitness sessions, meals, rest and study time. The hostel environment builds independence, discipline and camaraderie among players — qualities that translate directly to better performance on the cricket field.

Whether a player is from another city in Uttar Pradesh, a neighbouring state, or anywhere across India, our hostel facility makes it possible to access professional cricket coaching at YSCA year-round.

Hostel Facilities & Amenities

Safe Accommodation

Comfortable, clean living spaces designed for young athletes with proper ventilation, study areas and storage for personal belongings and cricket equipment.

Nutritious Meals

Three balanced meals daily plus snacks, designed to support athletic performance, recovery and growth. Vegetarian and non-vegetarian options available.

24/7 Supervision

Round-the-clock supervision by responsible staff ensuring discipline, safety and a structured daily routine for all residential players.

Year-Round Availability

The hostel operates throughout the year to support continuous training schedules, with no break during school holidays for committed players.

Training Convenience

Located near the academy grounds, eliminating daily commute stress so players can focus 100% on their cricket development and fitness.

Limited Rooms

Rooms are strictly limited to maintain quality standards, supervision ratios and individual attention for every residential player.
