RESIDENTIAL FACILITY

Cricket Academy Hostel in Greater Noida

Residential accommodation for outstation cricket trainees, with information for families considering training away from home.

Residential Cricket Training

The hostel is intended for outstation trainees who need a practical home base while they pursue cricket coaching in Greater Noida. Accommodation is planned around a player’s training, rest and everyday routine.

The published hostel plan includes twin- and triple-sharing rooms, individual storage and study space, common areas and Wi-Fi. Families can assess whether the residential setting is appropriate for the player’s age, education and support needs.

Meals are planned as breakfast, lunch and dinner with an evening snack. Residential enquiries should include the player’s age, programme interest, home location and expected stay.

Residential information for families

Accommodation can be useful for a player who lives a long way from a training ground. The academy publishes a hostel information page so families can start a residential enquiry with the right questions.

Before making arrangements

Families can consider the player’s travel needs, age, education and support network before deciding whether residential accommodation is appropriate.

Information to share

A useful enquiry includes the player’s age, home location, programme interest and any important day-to-day requirements. This helps the academy respond to the family’s question directly.

Details that need agreement

Room arrangements, meals, daily routine, supervision, parent communication and admissions terms are operational details. They should be discussed with the academy before a family makes a decision.
