Six Core Areas of Athletic Conditioning

Six core areas of physical development tailored for cricket performance.

Strength Training

Build functional cricket strength through targeted weight training, bodyweight exercises and resistance work — developing the power needed for explosive batting shots, fast bowling and powerful throws.

Speed & Explosiveness

Develop explosive acceleration for quick singles, boundary-saving sprints and rapid fielding reactions through sprint drills, plyometrics and reaction-time exercises.

Agility & Footwork

Improve change of direction, lateral movement and on-field responsiveness — critical for batting footwork, fielding agility and bowling run-up consistency.

Endurance & Stamina

Build the cardiovascular stamina to perform consistently through long match days, multi-day training blocks and demanding tournament schedules without fatigue affecting performance.

Mobility & Flexibility

Maintain full range of motion essential for bowling actions, batting movements and fielding positions. Proper mobility reduces injury risk and improves technique execution.

Cricket-Specific Conditioning

Workout plans designed specifically for the physical demands of competitive cricket — covering batting power, bowling endurance, throwing accuracy and fielding explosiveness.

Weekly Fitness Structure

How fitness sessions are structured throughout a typical training week.

Monday Upper Body Strength + Core Stability
Tuesday Speed & Agility Drills + Plyometrics
Wednesday Lower Body Strength + Explosive Power
Thursday Cricket-Specific Conditioning + Endurance
Friday Full Body Circuit + Functional Mobility
Saturday Recovery Session — Yoga, Guided Stretching, Light Cardio
