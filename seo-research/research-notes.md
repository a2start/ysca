# YSCA qualitative research notes

Research date: 29 October 2026

## Scope and limits

This is qualitative search-intent research, not keyword-volume research. Google Search Console and Google Keyword Planner were not available in this workspace, so no search volumes, difficulty scores, rankings, or traffic figures have been asserted.

The confirmed location used for the keyword map is the address already published in the repository: J-1, Block J, Delta II, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201310. Greater Noida is therefore the primary local target. Noida is treated only as a potential travel catchment; the site does not claim a Noida branch.

## Sources reviewed

- Google Search results for `cricket academy in Greater Noida` and `cricket net practice Greater Noida`, reviewed 29 October 2026.
- [Young Star Cricket Academy public result](https://youngstarcricketacademy.in/) — used only to identify public claims that need owner confirmation, because it does not consistently match this repository's intended `.com` canonical host.
- [SPM Cricket Academy](https://spm-cricket-academy.cricketground.in/) — illustrates that local ground searchers expect a clear location and an enquiry route.
- [Cricket Academies in Greater Noida](https://cricketacademys.com/cricket-academy-in-greater-noida/) and [CricHeroes local guide](https://blog.cricheroes.com/cricket-academies-in-greater-noida/) — used qualitatively to identify common comparison needs: location, age group, facilities, timings and fees.

## Findings applied

- Parents and players need the right level, location, contact method, timings, fees and trial availability before they can decide. The site should present verified answers or invite an enquiry; it should not promise unverified facilities, schedules, trial terms or outcomes.
- The contact form now uses allowlisted program/trial query parameters and tells visitors that the academy will confirm availability instead of treating a trial or response time as guaranteed.
- Existing keyword targets remain qualitative ideas in `keyword-research.csv`; its `Not available` volume fields must remain unchanged until a verified keyword-data source is connected.

## Owner confirmation needed before further copy expansion

Confirm the official domain, the exact Greater Noida address/postcode, actual service area, coaching credentials, age bands, net/ground features, hostel availability and inclusions, gym supervision/equipment, training times, fees, trial policy, and official social profiles. These are material claims, not SEO filler.
