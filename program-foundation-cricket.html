Program Overview & Methodology

The Foundation Cricket Program at Young Star Cricket Academy is tailored specifically for children aged 6 to 10 taking their very first steps into cricket. At this formative stage, training is centered around motor skill development, hand-eye coordination, balance, and learning fundamental bat-and-ball mechanics in a fun, safe, and positive sporting environment.

We utilize age-appropriate safety equipment, soft-touch leather balls, and incrediballs to build confidence and technique before transitioning to harder match balls. Our experienced grassroots coaches prioritize joy, teamwork, discipline, and building a genuine passion for cricket that lasts a lifetime.

Equipment Guideline: Comfortable sportswear and running/athletic shoes. Academy provides starter bats, balls, and protective gear during early trial stages.

Structured Curriculum & Training Syllabus

Progression-based training modules taught sequentially across the training term:

Module 1: Batting Fundamentals

Grip, balanced stance, backlift alignment, head position, and executing basic front-foot defense, straight drive, and cover drive.

Module 2: Bowling Mechanics & Action

Developing a smooth run-up rhythm, upright seam position, full circular arm motion, and non-stressful follow-through to prevent bad habits early.

Module 3: Fielding Reflexes & Agility

Two-handed ground pick-up, underarm & overarm target throws, catching technique with soft hands, and dynamic agility obstacle courses.

Module 4: Game Awareness & Team Spirit

Understanding cricket pitch dimensions, basic scoring and dismissal rules, calling between wickets, respecting teammates and umpires.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from parents and aspiring cricketers:

Is prior cricket experience required to enroll in this batch?

No prior experience is necessary. This program is created specifically for absolute beginners and focuses on building fundamentals from scratch.

Is hard-ball cricket played in this program?

Ask the academy about the equipment currently used for the relevant age group and what players should bring.

Can parents sit and watch their children train?

Yes, our Greater Noida facility features clean, shaded parent viewing galleries overlooking the turf nets and practice ground.
